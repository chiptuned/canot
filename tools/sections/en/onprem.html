Exit locally, to your own infrastructure.

No cloud needed at the destination: a folder is enough. Canot writes the copy in Apache Iceberg format to your disk, your NAS or an S3 storage you host, with the evidence file next to it.

uvx canot drill "snowflake://MY_ACCOUNT/MY_DATABASE?user=MY_USER" /data/iceberg
  • /path/to/folderLocal disk, network share or mounted NAS.
  • s3://bucket/canot?endpoint=https://s3.internal:9000Your S3-compatible storage (MinIO, Ceph, NetApp…), with an internal proxy and certificate authority if needed.
  • --catalog env:CANOT_CATALOG_URIA PostgreSQL catalog of your own; the password stays in an environment variable.

The copy does not depend on Canot: DuckDB, Spark and Trino read it, and the reread.sql file recounts every table without the tool. Details are in the documentation.
