Bring your data home, simply.

Changing hosts should not be an act of faith. Canot copies your data to where you want it to live, compares the copy to the original and hands you the evidence.

A European host

Scaleway, OVHcloud, Outscale, IONOS, STACKIT: one shortcut per host, or any S3-compatible storage. You choose, based on your sovereignty requirements.

On your premises

A folder, a NAS, internal S3 storage (MinIO, Ceph), with your own proxy and certificate authority. No cloud at the destination.

No dependency on Canot

The copy uses the open Apache Iceberg format: DuckDB, Spark and Trino read it. You can check it without us, and move off it whenever you want.

SecNumCloud qualification, when it matters to you, depends on the host's specific offering: check the ANSSI list. Canot certifies nothing.
