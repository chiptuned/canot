  • Identical
    Seven tables out of eight arrive identical.The 8 TPC-H tables were copied, then compared: row count, per-column statistics and a fingerprint of each table's content. The eighth differs only in the precision of one column, see below.
  • 3 of 4
    Three queries out of four return the same figures.Four TPC-H queries were replayed on both sides. Three return exactly the same result; the fourth reads every column of the largest table and reveals the gap below.
  • To fix
    One timestamp column loses precision.The source keeps nanoseconds; Apache Iceberg (version 2) stops at microseconds. The instant is identical to the microsecond. Canot added this column for the demonstration: on your data, the same gap shows up in nanosecond timestamp columns.
  • Signed
    Any change to the evidence is visible.Each step is recorded in a log signed with a key created on your side. If a line of the log changes, verification fails. The signature attests to the file's integrity, not to its author or its date.
