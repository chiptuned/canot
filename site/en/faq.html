Frequently asked questions.

What do you see of our data?

Nothing with the tool alone: it runs in your accounts and sends us nothing. During an engagement, we work with limited access that you grant, under a GDPR data processing agreement.

Is this a certification?

No. It is a set of measurements for your own assessment. It says nothing about whether your contracts or your organization meet any requirement.

Can you review our cloud contracts?

No, that is a lawyer's job. We record the facts (dates, notice periods, fees charged) and handle all the technical work. The legal analysis stays with your lawyer.

We don't want to leave our platform.

The drill does not make you leave. You keep your platform and get a measured exit cost to negotiate your renewal, plus proof that your exit plan has been tested.

How long does a drill take?

The copy and comparison take a few minutes on the sample datasets. On your scope, a full diagnostic takes three weeks, including the readout.
